Our Heart

A practice built on presence, faith, and real change.

New Vision Therapy & Wellness was founded by Candice Moffatt, LCMHC — a Rock Hill native and two-time Winthrop University graduate with more than 14 years of counseling experience. What began as one counselor’s calling has grown into a team of caring clinicians serving families across the Carolinas.

We believe healing happens when your whole story is honored — mind, body, and spirit. Faith is woven in gently, only when you want it, alongside proven, evidence-based care.
