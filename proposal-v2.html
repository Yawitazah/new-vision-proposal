What is a BAA, in plain language?

A Business Associate Agreement — the contract HIPAA requires between a provider and any vendor that touches patient data. It legally binds the vendor to protect that data and defines who’s responsible if something goes wrong. No BAA, no protection — regardless of how careful anyone is.

Why does this matter more for a therapy practice?

Psychotherapy notes get extra legal protection beyond standard medical records. Behavioral-health data is treated as higher-sensitivity because exposure can affect employment, custody, and relationships — and if substance-use treatment is ever involved, stricter federal rules stack on top with criminal penalties.

Do small practices really get fined?

Yes — small medical practices accounted for 55% of federal HIPAA penalties in 2022. Real small-practice examples: a treatment center fined $103,000 for missing basic safeguards; another settled at $10,000 over a single impermissible disclosure. We surface this so the value of doing it right up front is clear — not to alarm you.

What does the platform’s HIPAA add-on actually include?

A signed BAA, encryption of data at rest, mandatory multi-factor authentication, audit logging of who accessed what and when, and restricted platform-support access. It’s a permanent setting once enabled — and we obtain written confirmation that the AI features we use are within its coverage before go-live.
