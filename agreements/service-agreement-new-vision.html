Services Agreement

Between New Vision Therapy & Wellness, PLLC and Zah Brand Solutions

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of _______________, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Client: New Vision Therapy & Wellness, PLLC, a North Carolina professional limited liability company ("Client").

Provider: Zah Brand Solutions ("Provider").

1. Services

Provider will design, build, configure, and deliver a digital infrastructure system for Client (the "Build"), consisting of:

  • Website design and rebuild, published on Client's domain
  • Customer relationship management system and client pipeline architecture, including a structured client reassignment workflow
  • Email and text message automations, including reminders, no-show recovery, waitlist, and reactivation
  • AI receptionist for web chat and after-hours calls, including crisis escalation logic
  • Intake forms, booking calendars, and insurance verification workflows
  • Payment collection, invoicing, and no-show fee automation
  • Newsletter system with audience segmentation
  • HIPAA compliance configuration, go-live checklist, and live team training with written procedures

Client's clinical records system (SimplePractice) remains separate and is not replaced by the Build.

Setup and support, not day to day operation. Provider's role is to design, build, configure, and launch the system, to train Client's team to use it, and to remain available for ongoing support. Provider is not the operator of Client's day to day communications. Specifically, the email and text message automations (including reminders, no-show recovery, waitlist, and reactivation) and the newsletter system are delivered as configured, tested, working systems together with training and written procedures. Once delivered, Client's team writes, schedules, sends, reviews, and manages the ongoing messages, campaigns, and content. Provider remains available to advise, support, and troubleshoot. Any request for Provider to act as the day to day operator, to produce ongoing campaign or newsletter content, or to manage sending on Client's behalf is outside the scope of this Agreement and will be quoted separately in writing.

  2. Target payoff. The Parties intend for the Build Fee to be paid in full within approximately nine (9) months of the Effective Date. Client may prepay any amount at any time without penalty to shorten the payoff period.

3. Managed Service: No-Charge Period and Commitment Period

  1. Provider delivers managed services during the payoff period at no charge. From the date the system goes live until the Build Fee is paid in full, Provider provides the full managed service described in Section 1, including maintenance, updates, compliance monitoring, and support of Client's team, and does not bill Client any managed service fee for that period. Client's monthly payments during that period are applied entirely to the Build Fee.
  2. The no-charge period is permanent if the Commitment Period is satisfied. Provided Client satisfies the Commitment Period described below, Provider will never bill Client for the managed services delivered during the payoff period. The Parties acknowledge the intended economic effect: Provider delivers substantially more months of managed service than Client pays for. If the Build Fee is paid off in approximately nine (9) months, Client receives roughly twenty-one (21) months of managed service while paying for twelve (12). Client receives that benefit in exchange for, and conditional upon, the continuity of business represented by the Commitment Period.
  3. Managed service fee. Beginning the first full month after the Build Fee is paid in full, Client will pay Provider a managed service fee of $2,000 per month.
  4. Commitment Period. Beginning the first full month after the Build Fee is paid in full, Client commits to twelve (12) consecutive months of the managed service fee (the "Commitment Period"). This commitment is given in consideration of Provider delivering the full managed service without charge throughout the entire Build payoff period, a period expected to run approximately nine (9) months. Client may not terminate the managed service during the Commitment Period except as provided in Section 4.5.
  5. If the Commitment Period is not satisfied. If Client terminates this Agreement or materially breaches it before the Commitment Period is satisfied, the conditional no-charge benefit described in Section 3.2 is forfeited, and the early termination amounts set out in Section 4.3 become due.
  6. After the Commitment Period. Once the Commitment Period is satisfied, the managed service continues on a month to month basis, terminable by either Party upon thirty (30) days written notice, until the Parties execute a longer term managed service agreement. Renewal terms may be one year or longer as the Parties agree in writing. Neither Party is obligated to renew.
  7. The managed service fee covers ongoing management and support as described in Section 1. It does not make Provider the day to day operator of Client's communications, and it does not include new projects or custom builds, which will be quoted separately in writing.

4. Term and Client Commitment

  1. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated as provided herein.
  2. Client's commitment. Client commits to both of the following: (a) the payment schedule in Section 2 until the full $18,500 Build Fee is paid, and (b) the Commitment Period described in Section 3. The reduced deposit, and Provider's delivery of managed services at no charge throughout the payoff period, are provided in consideration of that combined commitment.
  3. Early termination or breach by Client. If Client terminates this Agreement, or materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice, before both commitments in Section 4.2 are satisfied, then Client will pay Provider:
    • (a) the entire remaining unpaid balance of the $18,500 Build Fee; plus
    • (b) if termination occurs before the Build Fee is paid in full, an amount equal to the number of whole months during which Provider delivered managed services without billing, multiplied by $2,000; or
    • (c) if termination occurs during the Commitment Period, an amount equal to the number of whole months then remaining in the Commitment Period, multiplied by $2,000. By way of example, if Client terminates with three (3) months remaining in the Commitment Period, the amount owed under this subsection is $6,000.
    These amounts become immediately due upon termination. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Provider delivered the full managed service to Client without charge throughout the Build payoff period, a benefit to Client valued at approximately $18,000, and did so in reliance on Client's twelve (12) month commitment under Section 3.4. The amounts in subsections (b) and (c) are a reasonable measure of the loss Provider suffers from early termination and are not a penalty. If Client satisfies the Commitment Period in full, no amount under subsections (b) or (c) is ever owed.
  4. Termination by Provider. At any time after the Build Fee has been paid in full, Provider may terminate this Agreement and the managed service for any reason upon thirty (30) days written notice to Client. If Provider terminates under this provision, Client owes nothing beyond amounts already accrued through the termination date, Client's remaining Commitment Period ends, and neither Party has any further liability to the other. Provider will furnish Client an export of Client's data as described in Section 6.
  5. Termination for Provider's failure to perform. Nothing in this Section limits Client's remedies if Provider materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice from Client.

5. Third Party Tools and Costs

  1. The system runs on third party tools that bill Client directly. These are not Provider's fees and Provider applies no markup to them.
  2. GoHighLevel: approximately $394 per month (Starter plan plus the HIPAA compliance add-on), beginning when the system goes live. Optional add-ons and usage based charges, including email, text, phone, and AI usage, are billed by GoHighLevel at its own rates.
  3. Claude account: Client will maintain its own subscription for the AI assistant used within the system. Cost depends on the plan selected by the Parties.
  4. Additional tools. Additional third party tools may be required as the Build progresses and may carry their own fees. Provider cannot identify every such fee as of the Effective Date. Provider will disclose the cost of any tool to Client before it is added to the system.

6. Managed Service Model, Data, and Platform

  1. The system is provided as a managed service. Client is not purchasing outright ownership of the underlying platform.
  2. Client data belongs to Client at all times. All client records and data that Client places into the system remain Client's property and Provider will furnish a usable electronic export upon Client's request.
  3. Provider builds, operates, maintains, and secures the platform on which the system runs.
  4. Upon termination of the managed service, Client retains its data and Provider will provide an export. Client's access to the Provider operated platform ends, and Client is responsible for migrating its data to another platform.

7. Protected Health Information

The Parties will execute a separate Business Associate Agreement governing the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information. That agreement must be signed before work begins and before any real client data enters the system. The Build will be constructed and tested using placeholder or de-identified data only until Client's protections are active.

8. Client Responsibilities

  1. Client will provide timely access to accounts, content, branding materials, and personnel reasonably necessary for the Build.
  2. Client will review and approve each phase of work within a reasonable period. Delays in Client review may extend the delivery timeline.
  3. Client is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of the content and data it supplies.

9. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential the non-public business information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, and will use it only to perform under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination.

10. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages. Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement will not exceed the total amounts paid by Client to Provider under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Nothing in this section limits either Party's obligations under the Business Associate Agreement or under applicable law.

11. General

  1. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
  2. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the Business Associate Agreement, is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and supersedes prior discussions and proposals.
  3. Amendment. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
  4. Independent Contractor. Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Client.
  5. Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.
